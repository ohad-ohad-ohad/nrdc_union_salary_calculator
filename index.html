How this number is built

    What we're asking for

    A raise for everyone

    Base pay for every job level goes up 5% a year for the life of the contract — and no one's own salary grows slower than that, whatever their level.

    Real growth inside your band

    Tenure compounds automatically, and meeting expectations earns a step promotion every year instead of a coin flip. Growth stops being a favor and becomes a rule.

    Pay for the work we actually do

    Litigation, critical site operations, and translation work carry real premiums — the same work, recognized the same way, for everyone who does it.
